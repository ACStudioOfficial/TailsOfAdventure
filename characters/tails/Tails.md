## Character’s bio: Tails
Name: Tails  
Nook ID: MLC-771  
Date of Birth: 16th of April 2004  
Sex: Male  
Height: 1.85 m (6'1)  
Description: Tails has an inflated sense of self-importance, bordering on a god complex. However, he's not entirely delusional and recognizes his limitations. This creates a humorous tension where he tries to act like the leader but sometimes makes mistakes. Despite his inflated ego, Tails is resourceful and can come up with creative solutions in a pinch. His luck has also aided the team in completing missions, even if it wasn't exactly by skill. His arrogance and lack of self-awareness often lead him into trouble. He can be insensitive, and his words can have unintended consequences. This is painfully evident when he accidentally hurts Wey, mentioning her mother. The incident with Wey serves as a turning point for Tails. He realizes the importance of their bond and starts to mature, becoming more self-aware and considerate, since they were together since 2004
