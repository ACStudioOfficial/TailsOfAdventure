## Character’s bio: Wey
Nook ID: MSC-772  
Date of Birth: 16th of July 2004  
Sex: Female  
Height: 1.80 m (5'11)
Description: Wey is a reserved character who doesn't speak much. When she does, it's usually directed at Tails, often in the form of playful teasing. She has known Tails for longer than she remembers due to her father deciding that Tails and Wey should grow up together due to complications with Wey's mother, who died 8 months after Wey's birth, as well as a long-standing friendship of both families. Wey's mother knew that she would not survive these complications and during these times, she tried to spend more time with Wey, and also leave as many photos and videos together as possible, which Wey treasures. Despite her quiet nature, Wey is perceptive and over the years, developed an ability to see through Tails' bravado. Her grounded personality helps balance the group dynamic. While Wey forgives Tails, the depth of the emotional wound remains and reveal itself in the last episode of season 1. Wey also seems to mature after the incident. She becomes more assertive and stands up for herself.
