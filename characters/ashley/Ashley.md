## Character’s bio: Ashley
Name: Ashley Henderson  
Nook ID: MTS-770  
Date of Birth: 3rd of November 2000  
Sex: Female  
Height: 1.83 m (6'0)
Description: Ashley was born in Vancouver, Canada. At the age of 7, her parents decided to send Ashley to a summer military camp, starting her military career. At age of 25, she was offered a job at Nook Inc. At that time, she had a rank of Brigadier General of Canada. While Ashley is introduced later in the season, she develops a close bond with the team, particularly worried about Tails' capture, because of her mistake with a previous team, when she was new in a mercenary program, resulting the death of the leader and team dissolution after that. This hints at her potential to become a core member. She shows her emotions very rarely
