## Character’s bio: Lenka
Nook ID: jMBC-774  
Date of Birth: 20th of August 2003  
Sex: Female  
Height: 1.76 m (5'9)  
Lenka is a unique character who, at first glance, seems to have traits resembling autism, but occasionally reveals flashes of intelligence that rival those of Ashley or Tails. She’s like a D100 dice roll—where 99 sides are failures, but the 100th is an absolute critical success. Most of her actions result in immediate failure, but when she does succeed, it’s in a way that’s remarkably overpowered, completely turning the situation on its head. Lenka is highly sensitive to harm or insults, but rarely shows it in the moment. It’s only later that she might “accidentally” let slip an inconvenient truth about someone, hinting that she never really forgets. Her decision-making is entirely independent, making her wildly unpredictable, one moment she might ruin what should have been a perfect day, and the next, she’ll transform a disaster into an unexpectedly great one. You never know when or how she’ll impact the situation, making her both a wildcard and a potential game-changer.
