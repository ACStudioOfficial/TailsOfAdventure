## Overall description
### Technical side
    
In this section, I will try to explain the general shape of “Tails of Adventure”: “Tails of Adventure” is technically a fanfiction about Animal Crossing, which gives to it a deeper lore, by making Nook Inc a multiverse company. This approach makes the whole structure “modular”: No matter what adventure I make, it could be explained by “it’s a multiverse” argument, but it doesn’t mean that I could make anything, and it will work. It’s like trying to build a Lego house: blocks are modular and could be connected in any way you want, but any “deviation” from the rules resulting either an unstable house or not a house at all. This is my 2nd observation about the lore: different “modules” make different connections, which must be accounted if we want the whole plot to be as full as possible. Maybe that’s understandable for you, but I want to give you my vision about the series. You vision could be different, but I am open for any contributions.

### Nook Inc  
As mentioned earlier, Nook Inc is a multiverse company and like any company, they have different positions: Tom Nook, being CEO of Nook Inc. Isabelle, being COO, etc... Timmy and Tommy Nook won’t be present in the series due to a strict rule: No characters under 18.  
Nook Inc has its own mercenaries, which also have their own system of positions. The mercenaries are the force that does all the work to support Nook Inc, starting from cleaning up the mess from the previous mercenaries to a literal elimination of important figures, like CEOs of companies, which doesn’t support Nook Inc. Usually, they’re doing some other tasks, like spying, being Nook Inc’s representatives, deliver some important cargo, guarding events and so on. Mercenaries are divided in 3 ranks: 3rd rank, just doing clean-ups from the works of the previous mercenaries. That rank was introduced to add realism to Nook Inc, by showcasing that there’s someone, who helps Nook Inc to cover their tracks. Those mercenaries are often “the worst”, since most of the beginners were given a 2nd rank. However, sometimes, those 3rd ranked mercenaries were given a 2nd rank missions, but they're usually simpler and those cases are rare. 2nd rank, however, is the most popular position in Nook Inc. They often doing some dirty work (like, retrieving intel), which would be a waste of time for 1st rank mercenaries. The 1st rank mercenaries are quite rare: only 1 out of 1.000.000 2nd rank mercenaries are promoted to the 1st rank. 1st rank mercenaries are the most skillful Nook Inc mercenaries, which handles the most difficult tasks.  

### Mercenary Program Architecture

To maintain operational stability and corporate oversight, the Mercenary Program is divided into two distinct functional branches: Management branch and the Execution branch.

3.1 The Management Branch (Supervisors)

Supervisors act as the "intermediate layers" between Nook Inc’s higher-level corporate leadership and the field assets.

Organizational Role: Supervisors are corporate staff, not field operatives. They reside in the management hierarchy and do not participate in combat or spying.

Core Responsibilities:  
* Translating vague corporate goals into actionable mission parameters for the teams.

* Providing equipment, intel, and logistics (transport, medical, extraction).

* Tracking the team's "health" and success rates.

* Supervisors are directly answerable to the Executive Board. If a team causes a major failure, the Supervisor is the one held liable for the poor planning.

Hierarchy: Indicated by the level "0" (e.g., MLC-770).

Supervisors hold full authority over mission data and strategic objectives. However, they are strictly prohibited from interfering with on-field tactics once an operation has started. On-field tactics are the sole responsibility of the Team Leader. However, a Supervisor acts as a Safety Monitor. They have the right to suggest or initiate an Emergency Extraction if telemetry indicates an existential threat to the team. In this case, the Supervisor’s "Abort" command overrides the Leader's tactical autonomy.

3.2 The Execution Branch (The Mercenaries)

This is the core of the program. These are the field-active units who executes commands of supervisors into results.

Organizational Role: The Force branch. These are the assets deployed into the field to perform tasks.

The Team structure (Standard 4-Unit group):  
* Leader (Level 1): The primary leader. Responsible for real-time decision-making and plan changing if the original plan fails.
* Secondary (Level 2): The primary redundancy. Ready to take over the "Master" role if the Leader's unavailable.
* Backup (Level 3): Secondary redundancy. Ensures the team remains operational even after two casualties.
* Backup Junior (Level 4): The "Recovery". Stays outside the combat zone. Their sole purpose is to ensure the team can be evacuated if the mission is compromised.  

Operational Rights: Unlike Supervisors, Mercenaries can use weapons, sabotage infrastructure, and neutralize targets to achieve the mission objective.

### ID system in Nook Inc:  
(j) - indicates that this mercenary is a “backup junior”  
M – stands for a “mercenary”. Different types of positions have different letters, like CEO having “G” or COO having “E”  
L – Indicates the profession itself: For the CEO it is “GD”, and for the COO it is “ED”. For mercenaries, it usually indicates that the mercenary is a leader, secondary or backup (“L”, “S”, “B” respectively)  
C – Just a random letter for an ID  
7 – 1 out of 2 hexadecimal UID (Unique Identifier)  
7 – 2 out of 2 hexadecimal UID (Unique Identifier)  
1 - shows the level of hierarchy in a selected group (9, being the lowest and 0, being the highest). For mercenaries its usually this one: 0 – supervisor, 1 - Leader, 2 – secondary, 3 - backup and 4 – backup junior  
Also, as the verification method, the team also gave a name, which is based on a random color (Red Team, Orange Team, Yellow Team, etc...)  

## Season 1 vision
This season is the beginning of “Tails of Adventure”, so Tails, Wey and Anton doesn’t even know what they want nor what they must do, so they just show up their personalities: Tails has a god complex (But not so bright as to consider Tails a complete loser), Wey says little, and if she does, it’s only to tease Tails like she’s used to, and Anton is impulsive. With these characteristics, they actually complete missions with the help of luck, but later, that “toxicity” in their group forces Tails to accidentally hurt Wey in the most painful way, by mentioning her mother, which leaves Wey in tears, and she runs away. Tails comes up to her door and asks her for forgiveness, which she doesn’t accept. Tails starts to list all the things they had done in a childhood, making Tails cry, showcasing that Tails and Wey come a long way together and leaving each other like that is not permissible. Eventually, Wey forgives Tails. From that point, Tails and Wey became more self-aware, while nothing happened to Anton. They continued completing missions, not using luck, but how they were intended. It seems that they finally got over and started to look more like a normal team. In the pre-last episode of the season 1, during a mission, Tails got captured and everyone started to get worried, especially Ashley and Wey. At the end of that episode, Tails finds a way how to connect to a team and asked them for forgiveness, since he was naive and childish. The rest of the teams does the same. In the last episode of the season 1, they launch an operation to save Tails. Meantime, Tails tries to escape by himself, and he was successful. After that, the whole team reevaluates their relationships.

## Season 2 vision:

EP 1-2: “Normal” Missions, Tension Bubbles
The crew starts the season more professional, they’re working like a real team, but something’s off: their missions feel too “clean,” too perfect. Tails notices they’re not being told what the missions are for anymore. Ashley gets strange messages from higher-ups. Oblique. Coded. Uncomfortable. Anton and Wey begin to clash philosophically, Wey trusts the system more than Anton does.

EP 3-4: The Forbidden Plan
A mission is assigned: recover a briefcase marked “N-13.” Nook Inc. says: “You are NOT to open this. Doing so will be considered treason.” They recover it. Mission goes well, but as they leave, they’re not paid immediately. This has never happened before. Anton quietly places a tracker in the case before turning it in.

EP 5: Division
Anton and Wey argue over ethics and secrecy. Tails stays quiet, disturbingly so. Anton’s tracker pings: the case was rerouted to a Nook Inc. underground facility. Ashley confesses to seeing schematics for something called “Sky Dust Protocol.” Lenka jokes, “What if we just committed a war crime without knowing?” The group starts secretly planning a break-in to retrieve what they gave away.

EP 6: The Real Betrayal
Ashley is torn between duty and loyalty. Wey pushes her to stay loyal. Tails says nothing. Anton hacks in, finds partial files. Project Sky Dust = Global communications shutdown via AI EMP grid. Lenka’s used as a distraction again (but it emotionally drains her this time -no one takes her seriously). They discover a ghost signature on the files, a signature from Snow.

EP 7-8: Nook’s Real Plan
They realize Nook Inc. knew about Snow all along. Nook Inc. is using him or being used. Ashley breaks them into a secure vault, finds logs of pre-recorded orders from “Chairman Nook,” whose physical location is unknown. The plans are a decoy: Project Sky Dust is real, but it’s already been activated, a countdown has started. Wey is confronted with the fact she supported this blindly. Tails finally breaks down: “I thought we were building something. We were just patching over rot.”
EP 9: “Broken Trust”
The crew fractures. Lenka refuses to follow orders. Anton wants to go public. Wey still wants to finish the mission. Tails suggests the unthinkable: go to Snow and bargain for intel. Ashley says no, but she can’t stop them. They split into 2 groups: Tails, Anton, and Lenka leave. Ashley and Wey stay.

EP 10: “The Meeting”
Tails meets Snow. Snow says, “I’m not your enemy. Your enemy is whoever let you believe you had a choice.” Snow reveals he leaked the decoy on purpose, he wanted to see if they’d do the right thing. He gives Tails a copy of the actual Sky Dust override code, but only if Tails agrees to broadcast Snow’s manifesto to the world. Tails agrees and doesn’t tell the others.

EP 11: “Before the Storm”
The crew reunites, strained but united. They plan their final move. Everyone thinks they’re saving the world. No one realizes Tails is about to broadcast Snow’s manifesto globally. Wey has a moment of doubt. Anton wants out. Ashley says “We’re too far in.” Lenka, scared but brave, makes a copy of the real plans and hides it.

EP 12 (Finale): “Echoes”
The plan works: Sky Dust is stopped, but Snow’s message plays across every major network -blaming the world governments, Nook Inc., and even the mercenary crews for complicity in global control. Chaos erupts worldwide. The crew stands over a burning facility. 
Anton: “What did we just do?” 
Tails (calmly): “We told the truth.”
